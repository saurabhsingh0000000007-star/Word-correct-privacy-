WORD CORRECT — Privacy Policy

SG Studio · Effective date: September 28, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SG Studio (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information in connection with the mobile game Word Correct (“Game” or “App”). We aim to collect only information reasonably needed to operate, secure, improve, and support the Game.

Important: This policy must be updated if the Game later adds services such as accounts, multiplayer, analytics, advertising SDKs, payments, or other data collection.

2. Information We May Collect

You may provide information when contacting us for support, such as your name, email address, and message.

Depending on enabled services, third parties may automatically process technical information such as device type, Android version, app version, crash/diagnostic data, advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, and usage information.

Word Correct does not intentionally collect precise GPS location unless the Game is later changed to request it and this policy is updated.

3. How We Use Information

  • Operate Word Correct and save game settings/progress where applicable.
  • Detect crashes, bugs, fraud, abuse, and security problems.
  • Improve gameplay and performance.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Show and measure advertising if enabled.
  • Process and verify purchases through applicable app-store/payment services.

4. Advertising

Word Correct may display ads through third-party advertising providers. If enabled, those providers may process device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location, and ad interaction information according to their own policies.

If SG Studio uses Google AdMob, Google’s applicable advertising and privacy terms also apply. Users can manage available advertising/privacy choices through the relevant platform and advertising settings.

5. Purchases and Payments

Word Correct may offer optional digital items such as virtual coins. Payments are processed by the applicable app store or payment provider unless checkout expressly states otherwise.

SG Studio does not intentionally collect or store full card numbers, CVV codes, UPI PINs, bank passwords, or similar payment credentials.

6. Virtual Coins and Game Data

Virtual coins and other digital items have no cash value outside Word Correct and cannot normally be exchanged for real money unless a feature expressly provides otherwise.

Scores, levels, settings, and similar game data may be stored locally or on a server when an online feature requires it.

7. Third-Party Services

The Game may use services for app distribution, advertising, payments, analytics, crash reporting, hosting, security, or authentication. Possible providers include Google Play services, Indus Appstore services, Google AdMob, and payment providers. Actual services may change as the Game develops.

8. Children and Teen Users

Word Correct is provided subject to applicable app-store age requirements and laws. We do not knowingly request unnecessary sensitive personal information from children. Where child or teen users are permitted, applicable child-privacy, advertising, and purchase requirements may impose additional restrictions.

Parents or guardians who have a privacy concern may contact us using the details below.

9. Data Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the Game, including hosting, analytics, advertising, payment, security, support, and app-distribution providers. Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, prevent fraud or abuse, enforce our terms, or protect rights and safety.

We do not sell personal information as a separate product.

10. Data Retention and Security

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, including operation, security, legal obligations, and dispute resolution. We use reasonable safeguards, but no electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure.

11. Your Choices and Requests

Depending on applicable law and the services used, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or information about personal data associated with you. You may stop using or uninstall the Game at any time.

12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game, services, or legal/platform requirements change. The updated policy will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
